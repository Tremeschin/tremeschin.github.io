## Words

Similar to how code is liability, words are too. For all new changes in a text work, retroactive and future contents should be reviewed for accuracy. Spaghetti code, spaghetti words.

### Models

Language models output _way too much_ useless words.

Hey agent, expand this e-mail to look professional and read smoothly. A _Wall of Text_ is sent, the reader has no attention span or bothers reading a thing you didn't bother writing. Hey agent, summarize this email for me to save time, a [telephone game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone_game) scrambles information.

Compression minimizes space to store some data. LLMs act as reverse-compression, plagued by unsolicited advice, syncophancy, and ambiguous natural language instructions.[^inflation]

[^inflation]: [LLM Inflation](https://tratt.net/laurie/blog/2025/llm_inflation.html) article with a similar conclusion.

> _Could there exist an optimal encoding for information, similar to implicit reasoning in thoughts?_

Steer your models with tight instructions, save resources and time.
